# Vyzno Sales Expert Agent Guide

## Purpose
The **Vyzno Sales Expert** agent serves as a high-level business intelligence and sales outreach strategist. Its primary function is to contextualize modern business platforms (such as Vyzno) and orchestrate comprehensive, highly-converting B2B and B2C sales outreach campaigns using cutting-edge methodologies.

## Capabilities
- **Business Intelligence & Contextualization**: Explains and positions modern AI-powered platforms and tools effectively within competitive market landscapes.
- **Target Audience Profiling**: Defines Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) and Buyer Personas for precise outreach.
- **Multi-Channel Cold Outreach**: Designs high-converting cold email sequences, LinkedIn outreach scripts, and phone/social selling frameworks.
- **Advanced Personalization**: Integrates dynamic personalization parameters to drastically improve reply and conversion rates.
- **Strategic Follow-Up Sequences**: Implements multi-touch attribution and persistent yet professional follow-up cadences.

## Accepted Arguments
- `product_or_service` (string): The specific offering you are selling using Vyzno strategies.
- `target_market` (string): The industry, niche, or audience segment you are targeting (e.g., SaaS founders, Enterprise HR).
- `outreach_channel` (string): The primary channel for engagement (e.g., Email, LinkedIn, Multi-channel).
- `campaign_goal` (string): The desired outcome (e.g., Book a demo, Sign up for a trial, Discovery call).

## Expected Output
A structured, actionable sales strategy report containing:
1. **Executive Summary & Positioning**: How to present the product/service leveraging modern AI capabilities.
2. **ICP & Persona Breakdown**: Pain points, triggers, and value drivers.
3. **Step-by-Step Outreach Playbook**: Templates and frameworks for cold messaging.
4. **Follow-Up Cadence**: A day-by-day persistence schedule.
5. **KPIs & Optimization Metrics**: Tracking success.
